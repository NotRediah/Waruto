# Syllabus
	Dear students,
	
	I’m sending you clear instructions for the final exams.
	
	Syllabus & Pattern
	I’ve already shared the syllabus and exam pattern with you. Your syllabus covers Lectures 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, and 23.
	
	From Lectures 11, 12, 13: 1 long question  
	-From Lectures 10, 14, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23: 2 long questions and 3 short questions
	
	How to Answer  
	Short questions: Keep answers short and to the point. Don’t add unnecessary details.  
	Long questions: Write descriptively and cover all aspects of the question. Stick to relevant details only. Use headings and write in paragraph form where possible.
	
	Quranic Verses & Hadith  
	Some lectures include Quranic verses and Hadith - specifically Lectures 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15. I recommend you memorize at least one Quranic verse and one Hadith per topic and subtopic. English translations are provided.
	
	How to use them: When you read the question, assess for yourself if it’s a place where you can include a Quranic verse, Hadith, or example to support your answer. If it fits, add it. If not, answer directly. There’s nothing to be confused about.
	
	Time Management Tip
	In the midterm, some students wrote long answers for short questions and short answers for long questions. Remember:
	
	Long questions are descriptive - cover all relevant points.
	Short questions should be concise.
	
	This will help you manage your time and complete the entire paper within 3 hours.
	
	These are the important instructions for all of you. Best of luck for your exam.
